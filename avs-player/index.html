If the connection needs help

Votre Ordinateur principal doit rester allumé avec le projet ouvert. Régénérez un lien temporaire expiré dans le launcher.

For a local network address beginning with http://, choose Open in tab. If a streamer appears but internet video fails, check the TURN settings in your launcher.

If an access or sign-in page blocks the embedded player, use Open in tab.
MAKE YOURSELF AT HOME

Choose your controls.

Touchscreen

Use the MOVE and LOOK pads in the player. The E and Space buttons mirror those keys. Toggle the pads with Touch controls.

Keyboard & mouse

Click inside the experience, then use your project's usual controls. Press Esc to release the cursor.

Gamepad

Connect your controller and press a button to activate it. Actions use your Unreal project's existing bindings.

Fullscreen gives you more space. On devices without browser fullscreen, the player expands to fill the page.
